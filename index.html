About me

I connect business goals with technical execution.

I’m a Technical Project Manager with three years of experience coordinating digital projects, managing client relationships and guiding development teams. My role isn’t to write the code. It’s to understand it well enough to make informed decisions, set clear expectations and deliver the right solution.

My work spans GoHighLevel CRM systems, WordPress websites, marketing automation, SaaS and AI-related platforms, online course platforms and e-commerce projects.

I turn client conversations into clear tasks, help developers move without guesswork, and check every deliverable against what was agreed.

My process

A clear path from requirements to delivery.

Six steps keep every project organised, transparent and moving forward.

  2. 01

    Discover

    Understand the client’s objectives, requirements, priorities and expected outcomes.

  3. 02

    Analyze

    Review technical requirements, identify dependencies, assess feasibility and discuss approaches.

  4. 03

    Plan

    Break requirements into clear tasks, set priorities and document acceptance criteria.

  5. 04

    Coordinate

    Brief developers, clarify requirements, resolve blockers and keep stakeholders updated.

  6. 05

    Review

    Validate completed work against requirements, collect feedback and coordinate revisions.

  7. 06

    Deliver

    Coordinate final delivery, confirm open items and keep documentation and timesheets up to date.
